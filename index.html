How it works

The 3D model above runs through the same cycle.

  1. Connect the water can

    A pipe at the back of the machine draws water from your water can.

  2. A glass drops

    Glasses drop onto the tray automatically, two at a time.

  3. It fills and stops

    Water fills the first glass and stops when it is full, then fills the second.

  4. Take your glass

    Both glasses wait on the tray. When one is taken, the next glass is filled.
